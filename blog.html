VATSIM has an issue

26/11/26

VATSIM's Code of Conduct has an issue in policy A4 that seemingly got forgotten or something idk

The VATSIM CoC (A4(a)) says: 

During the registration process with VATSIM, you shall provide your real, full (e.g. first and
last) name. Nicknames, callsigns or abbreviations are not permitted to be associated with an
account holder's registered account. VATSIM reserves the right, in its discretion, to require proof of
real name and proof of age from an account holder.

Now tbh this is honestly terribly defined (What even is a "real" name?)

But even worse: It's kinda transphobic tbh 3:

When we contacted the membership support team to get our account name changed from Ashton to Ashley, they said we needed actual ID.

So it's just legal name, right?

nope! As it turns out the VP of Membership is able to change the name of an account using their own descretion. [BOG 2021 Q2 Minutes]

So why is this feature not available without relying on the VP?

There was plans put into motion, as the Tech Team was working on an API to allow people to use a preferred name online [BOG 2021 Q3 Minutes],

But they stopped working on it by the next quarter [BOG 2021 Q4 Minutes], and nothing has been done since 2021 on the issue.


Despite this, we have decided to connect using our real name (Ashley) as opposed to Ashton, due to an unintended reading of A4(b)

A4(b)(4) states someone can connect with "Their real given name (e.g., Joseph)".

Now I don't know about you, but my name is much more real to me than my deadname.

And given it's my first/given name, I can connect to the network under Ashley instead of Ashton.

Is this a correct reading? Probably not. Have the supervisors complained in our private messages? No.

So at least for us it seems to work


But we shouldn't have to rely on rules lawyering the CoC to fly and control under our names

so VATSIM really needs to lock in and figure out a way to allow people to use their preferred names on the network.

It's not even a question of how to keep people accountable, because if banks can do it, surely VATSIM can too. Like come on mate it's just a name.
